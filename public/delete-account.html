🗑️ Delete Account & All Data

Last updated: August 20, 2025

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Please make sure to export any data you wish to keep before proceeding.

This page explains how to request deletion of your Expense Tracker: Budget & Spend account and all associated data. This applies to accounts created by MKN Labs.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account using either of the methods below:

Method 1 Delete via the App (Recommended)

If you still have access to the app on your device, follow these steps:

1 Open Expense Tracker: Budget & Spend

2 Go to Settings (profile icon in the top bar)

3 Navigate to Data Management

4 Scroll to the Storage & Maintenance section

5 Tap "Delete Account & All Data" (red button)

6 Confirm the deletion when prompted

This will immediately and permanently delete:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • All synced cloud data (transactions, budgets, categories, recurring rules, goals, payment methods, profile)
  • Device registrations from the connected devices list
  • Profile photos
  • All local data stored on your device

Method 2 Request via Email

If you no longer have access to the app (e.g., you switched devices or uninstalled the app), you can request account deletion by emailing us:

📧 Send Deletion Request

Please include your email address associated with the account and your Google Play order ID (if available) to help us locate your account. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.

2. What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

Data Type Where Stored Status
Firebase Authentication account (email, password hash, UID) Firebase Auth Deleted
Transactions (amount, category, notes, dates) Firebase Firestore + Local DB Deleted
Budgets (category, limits, periods) Firebase Firestore + Local DB Deleted
Recurring Rules (frequency, amounts, anchors) Firebase Firestore + Local DB Deleted
Goals (targets, progress) Firebase Firestore + Local DB Deleted
Categories & Payment Methods (custom entries) Firebase Firestore + Local DB Deleted
User Profile (display name, photo) Firebase Firestore + Local Storage Deleted
FCM Push Tokens (device registrations) Firebase Firestore Deleted
App Settings (preferences, data stores) Local DataStore Deleted
Local Backups Local Storage Deleted

3. What Data Is Retained

The following data may be retained for the specified periods after account deletion:

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Server access logs Up to 30 days Security auditing and fraud prevention
Aggregated, anonymized analytics Retained indefinitely Cannot be linked to individual users; used for product improvement
Crash reports Up to 90 days Firebase Crashlytics retains anonymized reports; cannot be used to identify you
Email correspondence Up to 2 years Support request history and legal compliance

4. Before You Delete

💡 Export your data first! You can export your transactions and financial data as a JSON file or SQLite database backup before deleting your account. Go to Settings → Data Management → Export Data in the app.

After deletion:

  • You will be signed out of the app immediately
  • All cloud sync will be disconnected
  • Your Pro Pass subscription (if any) will not be affected at the Google Play level — manage subscriptions through Google Play Subscriptions
  • You can create a new account at any time after deletion

5. Data Safety Summary

This information is provided for Google Play Data Safety compliance:

  • App: Expense Tracker: Budget & Spend
  • Developer: MKN Labs
  • Data collected: Financial transactions, budgets, user profile (name, email), device identifiers (for sync)
  • Data deletion: Users can delete all data at any time via the in-app feature or by emailing us
  • Data encryption: In transit (TLS) and at rest (Firebase)
  • Data sharing: We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes
