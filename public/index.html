Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Expense Tracker: Budget & Spend mobile application.

Transparency Note: We value your privacy. This app follows a "Privacy First" model, allowing full anonymous usage while providing secure cloud backup for users who choose to create an account.

1. Information We Access and Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may access the following information:

  • Financial Data: Your transactions, categories, budgets, and recurring rules. This data is stored in an encrypted local database (Room).
  • Account Information: If you sign in via Google or Email, we collect your unique User ID (UID), email address, and name/profile picture (for social login) to manage your account.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect a unique device ID (Android ID) strictly to enforce our security limit of 4 synchronized devices per premium account.
  • Device Hardware: We access the device model name (e.g., "Pixel 7") to help you manage your connected devices in settings.

2. Data Usage and Synchronization

Guest Mode (Offline): In Guest Mode, all financial data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Cloud Synchronization: For authenticated users, data is securely synchronized with Google Firebase (Firestore). This allows you to restore your data if you lose your phone or switch to a new device. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

3. Security and Permissions

  • Biometric Data: The App offers biometric lock features. Authentication is handled entirely by the Android system. The App never sees, collects, or stores your actual fingerprint or face templates.
  • App Permissions: We request notification permissions to provide you with daily reminders and budget alerts. These are processed locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We use industry-standard providers to ensure app stability and monetization:

  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID to show personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, cloud storage, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and remote configuration.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We believe you should have full control over your data:

  • Local Deletion: You can clear all local data at any time via the "Data Management" section in settings.
  • Full Account Deletion: Authenticated users can use the "Delete Account & All Data" feature in settings. This will permanently wipe your transactions from our cloud servers and delete your identity from our authentication records.
  • Web Requests: If you no longer have access to the app, you may request data deletion by emailing us at the address below.
