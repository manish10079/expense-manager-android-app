Placement & Flow Design

  • Exact Flow Placement: Inserted immediately before AuthContent (Sign In / Continue as guest).
  • Equal Gate Enforcement: Bound to app usage rather than login, ensuring guest users cannot bypass legal acceptance.
  • No Skip Allowed: The SKIP button is intentionally omitted from this screen, leaving only PREV to go back.
  • No Big Illustration: Large visual graphic dropped to grant ample comfortable reading space for terms and highlights.

Persistence & State Model

  • Local Persistence: Stored in AppSettingsDataStore as:
    • termsAcceptedVersion = "1.0.0"
    • termsAcceptedTimestamp = 1790640000000L
  • Zero Repeat Prompts: Once accepted, subsequent app launches bypass this step directly to MainScreen.
  • Ongoing Access: Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy remain readily accessible under Settings -> About.

Theme & ColorScheme Alignment

  • Dark Theme Tokens: Field #0A0A0A, Cards #141418 & #1E1E23, Accent Ink #9E84FF, CTA #5838FA -> #3713EC.
  • Light Theme Tokens: Field #F7F8FA, Cards #FFFFFF & #F1F2F4, Accent Ink #6A4DFF, CTA #6A4DFF -> #5B45D6.
  • Dynamic Glow: Button shadow elevated with 34% alpha brand accent glow when active, seamlessly switching to disabled slate when unchecked.
