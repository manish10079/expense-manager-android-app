gradient Light-Mode Cash Flow Hero — Variant Comparison

Dark (top-left) is the reference and is unchanged. Each card below sits on the real page background so you can judge separation, not just the card. Contrast shown for every ink.

Reading the comparison
  • Dark wins when the card is different from the page around it. Light variants succeed only when that separation is visible at a glance.
  • V1 is the softest; separation depends almost entirely on the border and shadow.
  • V2 gives the page a clear violet cast — the closest to dark's presence while staying light.
  • V3 flips to white ink and matches the language of your premium/Pro Pass cards; boldest and most "pro".
  • V4 is the safest: white card, one purple bloom, no gradient wash across the content.
  • Contrast is measured against the darkest point of each gradient. Amounts (20px bold) only need 3:1; labels and date need 4.5:1.
