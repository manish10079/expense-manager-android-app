Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Expense Tracker: Budget & Spend mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Offline Nature: The App is designed to be primary offline. Your financial data, including transactions, categories, and budgets, is stored locally on your device using an encrypted database (Room/SQLite). We do not have access to your financial data.

Personal Data: During onboarding, you may provide your name, gender, and date of birth. This information is stored strictly on your device to personalize your experience and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. Security and Permissions

Biometric Data: The App offers biometric lock features (Fingerprint/Face ID). The authentication is handled entirely by the Android system. The App never sees, collects, or stores your biometric templates.

Notifications: The App requests permission to send notifications for daily reminders and budget alerts. These are triggered locally by the App's internal logic.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, to show personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Backup

The App allows you to create manual or automated backups of your data. These backups are stored in your device's local storage or a location you choose. You are responsible for the security of these backup files.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
