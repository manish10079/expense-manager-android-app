# What's New

Every update to Expense Tracker, in plain words.

## Version 2.58 — August 2026

### The app now works in any direction
- **You can rotate your phone** — turn it sideways and the app rearranges itself to look great, instead of forcing you to hold it upright.
- **Big screens look like they were made for them** — on tablets and in landscape, the menu moves to the side and screens show more at once (like your balance card next to your recent transactions).
- **No more losing your place** — if you rotate the phone mid-way through adding a transaction, your half-filled form is still there.

### Easier on the eyes
- **Bigger system text doesn't break anything** — cards grow taller instead of cutting off words, and buttons stay big enough to tap.
- **No clutter in landscape** — the status bar hides when you turn the phone sideways, so you get more screen.

### Home screen, made nicer
- In landscape, the top row now shows: the Home button and your greeting together on the left, and the settings icon + your profile picture on the far right. Recent transactions sit right below, beside your balance cards.

### Fixed
- Charts no longer get cut off at the edges in landscape.
- The calculator's number buttons no longer disappear when you rotate the phone.

## Version 2.57 — August 2026

### Added
- A friendly **hand-wave animation** and a spinning settings icon when you open the app.
- **Fewer junk SMS imports** — promotional messages and loan-marketing texts are filtered out so they don't show up as transactions.

### Changed
- Small polish on the Home screen, the tip card, and the calendar heading.

## Versions 2.53 – 2.56 — August 2026

### Added
- **A proper notification center** — choose which alerts you get (big transactions, weekly summaries, savings-goal milestones, recurring bills, backups, and sync issues), and see details about each one.
- **Recurring bill reminders** — get nudged 7, 3, and 1 day before a bill is due, and on the due date itself.
- **Smarter budget alerts** — you're warned when you've spent 75% and 90% of your budget, and big alerts stand out more than small ones.
- **Pro users get extras** — some notification options are locked behind the Pro plan.
- The app now tells you when a backup fails or a sync is taking a while.

### Fixed
- Some reminders fired at the wrong times; weekly-summary time selection and a few date formats are now correct.

## Version 2.52 — August 2026

### Added
- **Daily reminders you can control** — set and manage them from the app.

## Version 2.51 — August 2026

### Added
- **Better budget warnings** — alerts come in levels, and related ones are grouped so they're easier to read.
