How to read this

  • Both frames are the same markup with a different theme class. The sheet is pinned to the bottom of a 412 × 915 screen, the edit profile screen behind it is dimmed by the scrim, and the whole stack fits: nothing scrolls.
  • The option rows are real buttons. Click one and the selection moves in both frames — the row, its badge, its label weight and the trailing "Selected" marker all follow.
  • Where the two supplied sheets genuinely differ, the difference is kept and driven by the theme class rather than smoothed over — see the table below.
  • One deliberate adaptation: the light supply is drawn in a 390 × 844 phone frame. It is shown at the sibling mockups' 412 × 915 Pixel 7 so the two can be compared side by side, which only moves the sheet a few pixels down the taller screen.
  • The non-binary glyph follows the dark supply's proportions in both frames; the light supply draws the same symbol a few pixels lower on its circle.
