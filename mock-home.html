What to check
  • On Home the colored category icons appear only in the Recent Activities rows — the tile is the one element that takes the category pair, exactly as in the app.
  • The Cash Flow hero now uses one shared recipe in both modes: hue 255, 47% saturation, the same two-bloom structure, the same inset surfaces, and the same ink colors. Only the base gradient's lightness changes (#271B4B→#120C22 in dark; #48328B→#2B1E52 in light). That is what makes the two read as the same card, recolored — not two different designs.
  • The two small cards (Spent Today / My Goals) keep the brand purple icon, because they are app actions, not categories.
  • Watch whether the colorful transaction tiles feel cohesive next to the purple hero, or whether they pull attention away from it.
