## 2. Feature Tiering & Monetization Model

ExpenseTracker employs a **Hybrid Freemium** model (Ad-Supported + Subscription + One-time).

### 🟢 Tier 1: Free (Standard Utility)
*   Manual transaction entry and balance tracking.
*   Basic dashboard (Daily/Weekly/Monthly totals).
*   PIN & Biometric Lock.
*   Category-wise spending (Current month only).

### 🟡 Tier 2: Ad-Supported (Feature Gating)
Users can watch a **Reward Video Ad** to unlock these features for **2 hours**:
*   **Advanced Filters:** Filter by specific Category or Payment Method.
*   **Security Plus:** Blur in recents, block screenshots, and custom auto-lock timers.
*   **Insights:** Yearly analytics distribution and Top 10 spending lists.
*   **AI Tips:** Dynamic spending advice based on habits.

### 💎 Tier 3: Premium (Pro & Power User)
Requires a subscription or one-time purchase. Unlocks all features permanently:
*   **Automation:** Recurring transactions for bills, salary, and rent.
*   **Data Safety:** Cloud Sync (Multi-device) and Automatic Scheduled Backups.
*   **Advanced Analytics:** Trend projections and category spending forecasts.
*   **Ad-Free:** Zero interruptions across the entire application.

## 5. Growth & Optimization Tactics

### 5.1 The "Ad-to-Pro" Funnel
*   **Trigger:** If a user unlocks ad-gated features 3+ times in a week, show a "Pro Upgrade" prompt.
*   **Message:** *"You've watched 3 ads this week. Upgrade to Pro for just ₹99/mo to save time and unlock Cloud Sync."*

### 5.2 Launch & Onboarding
*   **Introductory Offer:** 50% discount on the first year (₹399) if purchased within the first 10 minutes of app usage.
*   **Free Trial:** Mandatory 7-day free trial on the Yearly plan to reduce initial friction.

### 5.3 Regional Pricing (Global)
*   **USA/EU:** Use standard tiering (e.g., $4.99/mo, $39.99/year, $59.99 Lifetime).
*   **Emerging Markets:** Match the Indian "Sachet" model (low-cost monthly/weekly plans).
