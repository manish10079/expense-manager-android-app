The fix is a lightness gap, not a new hue: under red-green deficiency only blue↔yellow survives, so the second blue must sit far from the brand purple in lightness. Dark gains a pale sky, light a deep navy, giving 14.2 and 17.9.

Every lighter amber that would separate from red fails 3:1 on its own track, so this triple can never be made CVD-safe by hue alone. Two changes are therefore mandatory rather than optional: the over state drops to red-900 #7F1D1D, and every bar carries its state in text (“on track” / “near limit” / “over”). That is WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color — the meaning must not rest on the hue. The same applies to income and expense amounts: a +/− sign or an arrow is required, since income vs expense is only ΔE76 11.5 apart for protanopes in light.

Premiumunchanged — dark cardbrand exception
Purple budget, per screen. Field and cards carry zero chroma; the accent appears only as the hero rail and bloom (~9% of that card), the FAB (~2%), the active nav pill (~3%), the selected chip (~2%) and goal progress (~2%). That totals 15–25% of a screen, inside the 20–30% target — and the old goal bar, which was 100% purple, is now one purple among five tones.
